# Day 34 Short 2: Is the AI Boom Repeating the 2000 Crash?
THREAD: Today's concentration, the dot-com story, back to today (standalone Short, storytelling)

## CHAPTER 1: Short
Seven companies. Thirty-one percent of the S&P five hundred.
That's what Morgan Stanley found in mid twenty twenty-four.
And some analysts warn it could end like one famous crash.
Go back to nineteen ninety-five. The internet is brand new.
Over the next five years, the Nasdaq rises six hundred percent.
In December nineteen ninety-six, Fed chair Alan Greenspan goes on TV and warns about irrational exuberance.
Stocks keep climbing anyway.
By two thousand, the CAPE ratio, a common measure of how expensive stocks are, hits its all-time high.
Then the bubble bursts.
By October two thousand two, the Nasdaq is down seventy-eight percent.
Now look at today again.
Is this two thousand all over again?
