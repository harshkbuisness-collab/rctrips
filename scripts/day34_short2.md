# Day 34 Short 2: The Nasdaq Rose 600%... Then Lost 78%
THREAD: The dot-com bubble, and today's market (standalone Short)

## CHAPTER 1: Short
The Nasdaq rose six hundred percent.
Then it lost seventy-eight percent.
That was the US tech index, from nineteen ninety-five to its peak in March two thousand.
In December nineteen ninety-six, Fed chair Alan Greenspan warned about irrational exuberance.
But stocks kept climbing for three more years.
By two thousand, the CAPE ratio, a common measure of how expensive stocks are, hit its all-time high.
Then the bubble burst.
By October two thousand two, the Nasdaq was down seventy-eight percent from its peak.
By mid twenty twenty-four, Morgan Stanley said just seven tech companies made up thirty-one percent of the S&P five hundred.
Does that sound familiar?
