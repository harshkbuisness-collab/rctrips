## CHAPTER 7: The final answer
Finally, history tends to become neat after the fact. Commentators can draw arrows on a past chart and explain exactly why investors should have sold. Real-time decisions were harder because inflation, oil prices, policy, and company earnings were uncertain.

A disciplined investor should learn from past valuation mistakes without pretending future outcomes are obvious. Good investing is less about predicting when everyone else will change their mind and more about owning a portfolio that can survive when they do.
