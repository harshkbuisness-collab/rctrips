## CHAPTER 2: What Inflation Really Is
[calm] Inflation means prices, on average, are going up. When that happens, each dollar buys a little less than before.

## CHAPTER 3: Too Much Money, Too Few Things
[calm] Economists usually describe three causes of inflation. Think of them as three ways the shopping cart gets more expensive.

The first is too much demand. When lots of people want to buy, and there aren't enough things, sellers can raise prices.

Picture a concert with one thousand tickets and five thousand fans. The ticket price goes up, even though the concert stayed the same.
> VISUAL: A40_narrator_microphone slides in from the left; C28_crowd_small drops in; H17 price tag pops in "SOLD OUT"; E45_arrow_up_big pops in

## CHAPTER 6: How the Fed Fights Back
