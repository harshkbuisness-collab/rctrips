## CHAPTER 2: Your money is not in the vault
[calm] When Rosa deposits one thousand dollars, she imagines it sitting in a vault with her name on it. It is not.
> VISUAL: E01 cash stack slides in from the left; E21 safe vault pops in; red X on E21; A07 narrator confused

## CHAPTER 4: The safety net

## CHAPTER 7: How to protect your money
[calm] So what can Rosa, and you, actually do? Here are four simple steps.
