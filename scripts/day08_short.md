# Day 08 Short: Why US House Prices Didn't Crash
THREAD: Rates nearly tripled, so why didn't prices fall? (standalone Short, own script)

## CHAPTER 1: Short
Mortgage rates nearly tripled, so US house prices should have crashed.
They didn't.
The typical new house still sells for four hundred ten thousand seven hundred dollars.
Here's why.
Nearly all fifty million American mortgages have fixed rates.
And many were signed when rates hit record lows.
Sell today, and the same three hundred thousand dollar loan costs about eight hundred dollars more, every month.
So owners don't sell.
The Federal Housing Finance Agency counted it: one point three three million home sales that never happened.
Fewer sellers means fewer houses for sale.
Higher rates pushed prices down three point three percent.
But the missing sellers pushed them up five point seven.
So the market didn't crash. It froze.
Would you give up a three percent mortgage to move?
