## CHAPTER 3: The nineteen seventies: the slow burn
[calm] The nineteen seventies did not have one big crash. They had something sneakier. Prices rising fast, while the economy barely grew.
> VISUAL: E25 calendar flips "1970s"; E37 cart prices up pops in; E54 snail walks in; A35 narrator sweating
Economists call this stagflation. Stagnation plus inflation. A British politician described it as the worst of both worlds.

> SCREEN: Wikipedia, Stagflation | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stagflation | highlight "We now have the worst of both worlds"
The oil crisis of nineteen seventy-three was a big part of it. Oil prices jumped, and so did almost everything else.

## CHAPTER 8: What it means for you
[calm] Nothing is one hundred percent safe. Cash can lose to inflation. Gold can be banned or crash after a peak. Bonds can lose when rates jump. Houses can crack. Stocks can take decades.
> VISUAL: D03 dollar melting pops in; E05 gold bars; D24 bond document; D12 house cracked; G11 stock exchange; A35 narrator sweating
