## Fonts (free, Google Fonts / Canva)
- **Anton:** headlines and captions, always UPPERCASE
- **Inter Bold/ExtraBold:** labels, badges, brand name

## Video rules
1. Fixed every video: colours, fonts, caption style, logo placement, end card.
2. Changes every video: layout, scene types, demo type, animations, hook format.
3. First frame shows the hook text and the demo. No intro animation.
4. Something changes every 1–2 seconds.
5. Only 1–2 yellow words per line.
6. Word-by-word Anton captions, low but above Instagram's buttons.
7. Counter (1/3, 2/3…) on list videos.
8. Same end card on every video ("Follow for a free AI tool every day"; add "Comment FREE…" from week 3).
9. Red = paid, yellow = free.

## Logo prompts used

### Prompt 1: Icon only
A minimal flat vector logo icon for a tech brand called "FreeStack AI" that shares free AI tools. The icon shows three stacked layers seen in isometric view, like a stack of flat tiles. The top layer is a solid bright yellow (#FFE500) diamond shape. The two layers below are simple thick white chevron outlines, with the bottom one slightly faded grey. Clean geometric shapes, rounded corners, bold thick lines, perfectly centered. Solid matte black background (#111111). No text, no letters, no gradients, no shadows, no 3D effects, no extra decorations. Modern, simple, highly recognizable even at very small sizes, suitable for an Instagram profile picture. Flat 2D vector style, high contrast.

### Prompt 2: Full logo with text
A minimal flat vector logo for a brand called "FREESTACK AI". On the left, an icon of three stacked isometric layers: the top layer is a solid bright yellow (#FFE500) diamond, the two layers below are thick white chevron outlines, the bottom one faded grey. On the right, the wordmark "FREESTACK" in a tall, bold, condensed uppercase font similar to Anton, where "FREE" is bright yellow (#FFE500) and "STACK" is white, with a small grey "AI" underneath. Solid matte black background (#111111). Flat 2D, high contrast, no gradients, no shadows, no extra elements. Clean, modern, bold, tech brand style.

### Prompt 3: Instagram profile picture
A minimal flat vector app icon for "FreeStack AI". Centered inside a circle-safe area: three stacked isometric layers, the top one a solid bright yellow (#FFE500) diamond, the two below thick white chevron outlines, the bottom one faded grey. Solid matte black background (#111111). Large icon filling about 60% of the frame with even padding on all sides, so nothing gets cut off when cropped to a circle. No text, no gradients, no shadows. Simple, bold, readable at tiny sizes.
