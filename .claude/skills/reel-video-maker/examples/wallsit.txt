How long can you hold a wall sit? This simple test shows how strong your legs really are. Back flat against a wall. Slide down until your knees are at ninety degrees. Under thirty seconds: weak. Thirty to sixty: average. One to two minutes: strong. Over two minutes: very strong. To improve, do three holds a day and add five seconds every few days. Comment your time.
