You've been doing curls for months, and your arms still look skinny in a t-shirt. You're training the wrong muscle. And the fix is two exercises. Your biceps are only about one third of your upper arm. The other two thirds is your triceps, the back of your arm. That's the muscle that makes arms look thick, even when you're not flexing. So add these. Diamond push-ups: hands together under your chest, elbows tight. And chair dips: hands on a chair behind you, lower down, press up. Three sets each, close to failure, three times a week. Send this to the friend who only does curls. Because you've been doing curls for months...
