You do glute bridges, but you feel them in your lower back, not your glutes. That means your back is doing the work. And two small changes fix it today. Most people push their hips up by arching their lower back. Your glutes barely switch on. So do this. Lie on your back, feet flat, close to your hips. First, tuck your pelvis, like you're flattening your lower back into the floor. Then push through your heels and lift your hips, keeping that tuck.
