You do push-ups every day, and your chest still looks flat. It's not the number. Fifty, a hundred, it doesn't matter. And here's the real answer in ten seconds. Your chest grows when the last few reps of a set are a real struggle. A hundred easy push-ups do less than three hard sets. So here's what you need. Three to four sets, three times a week.
