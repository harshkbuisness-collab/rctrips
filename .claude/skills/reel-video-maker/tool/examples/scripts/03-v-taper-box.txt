Your body looks like a box. Same width at the shoulders, same width at the waist. It's not your genetics. And you can change your shape at home, with no gym. A V-taper comes from two muscles. Wide side delts on top, and wide lats on your back. Most people train chest and abs, and skip both. So here are the three exercises. Pike push-ups for your shoulders. Table rows for your lats: lie under a sturdy table and pull your chest up. And backpack lateral raises, slow, until it burns. Three sets each, three times a week. Send this to the friend who only trains chest. Because right now, his body looks like a box...
