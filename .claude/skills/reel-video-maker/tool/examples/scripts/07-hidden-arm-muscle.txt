Your biceps look okay when you flex, but from the side, your arm looks thin. There's a hidden muscle under your biceps. And one simple change to your curls trains it. It's called the brachialis. It sits underneath your biceps, and when it grows, it pushes your biceps up and makes your whole arm look wider from every angle. So here's how to train it. Hammer curls. Hold a water jug or a loaded backpack with your palm facing in, like holding a hammer, and curl it up. Lower slowly. Twelve to fifteen reps per arm, three sets, twice a week.
