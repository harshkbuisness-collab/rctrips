You train your abs, but your stomach still looks flat and plain from the front. You're missing the muscles on the sides. And training them the wrong way can make your waist look wider. Your obliques frame your abs. Strong, defined obliques make your whole midsection look sharper. But heavy side bends with weights can thicken your waist. So train them like this. Side planks: hold your body in a straight line on one forearm, thirty seconds each side. Then slow bicycle crunches: elbow to the opposite knee, fully extend the other leg. Twenty reps.
