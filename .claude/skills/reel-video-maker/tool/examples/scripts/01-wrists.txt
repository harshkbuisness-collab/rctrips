Your wrists look skinny, even when the rest of you is getting bigger. It's not just your bones. And the fix takes two exercises you can do with a water bottle. Your wrist bone can't grow. But the muscles right above it can, and almost nobody trains them directly. So do this. Sit down, forearms on your thighs, palms up, holding a full water bottle. Curl your wrist all the way up, then all the way down. Fifteen to twenty reps. Then flip your palms down and do the same. Three sets each, three times a week. Send this to the friend who hides his wrists in every photo. Because your wrists look skinny...
