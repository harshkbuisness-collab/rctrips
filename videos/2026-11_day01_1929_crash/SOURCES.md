## Facts in the script

- Dow -13% (Black Monday) and -12% (Black Tuesday); six-fold rise 1921-1929; 89% below peak by 1932; recovered Nov 1954: Federal Reserve History, *Stock Market Crash of 1929*.
- Money supply fell nearly 30% (fall 1930 to winter 1933); the Fed failed as lender of last resort: Federal Reserve History, *The Great Depression*.
- Bernanke quote: federalreserve.gov, remarks of 8 Nov 2002.
- FDIC limit $250,000: fdic.gov, *Understanding Deposit Insurance*.
- 16,410,030 shares on 29 Oct 1929: The New York Times front page, 30 Oct 1929.
- Still to double-check against a second source before upload: brokers' loans of about $8.5B; the Model T price $850 to $260; 1,028 economists' letter; about 9,000 bank failures; Bonus Army about 20,000 veterans; 25% unemployment (1933).
