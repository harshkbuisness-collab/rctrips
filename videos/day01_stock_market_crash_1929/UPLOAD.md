## Title
The 1929 Crash Didn't Cause the Great Depression. This Did.

Backup: How the 1929 Stock Market Crash Broke America

## Description
In 1929, the US stock market lost almost 90% of its value. But the crash alone didn't cause the Great Depression. What came next did: thousands of banks failed, the money supply shrank by a third, a trade war shut down world trade, and 1 in 4 Americans lost their job.

In this video, we follow the whole story with real charts and real photos: the Roaring Twenties boom, buying stocks with borrowed money, Black Tuesday, the bank runs, the Dust Bowl, and why it took until 1954 for the stock market to recover.
