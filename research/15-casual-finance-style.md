# Casual Finance style (owner's analysis tool, 25 Oct 2026)

What we take (fits our rules):
- Hook 0:00-0:45: show the headline/belief, then disprove it with one real number on screen in 30-45 s.
- "3-domino" chain: cause -> mechanical step -> forced action. Signpost: "The first domino...", "And this taught us two things".
- Punchy lines, 5-10 words, calm deadpan voice, ~140-160 wpm. Dry joke every 45-60 s.
- Visual change every 2-4 s (we already do ~2 s with pace_beats.py). Pop SFX on every new item, cash sound on money items.
- Very quiet lo-fi music bed (about -24 dB), never over the voice.
- Evidence: real charts with drawn lines; headline screenshots with yellow highlight / red circle.
- Title formulas: "The $X [Market] Nobody Sees Breaking" / "This [Boom] Is Setting Up Something Ugly" /
  "[Smart money] Is Quietly Doing [Opposite]".

What we skip:
- Meme clips (Jim Cramer, Family Guy): copyright risk. Use our own narrator reactions instead.
- White background and ElevenLabs: we keep our green brand and Kokoro af_heart.
- The Saurabh Bhatt Hinglish prompt: it is for a Hindi creator channel, not our US English channel.
