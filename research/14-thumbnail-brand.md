# Dollar Diaries thumbnail template (from the Day 1 pick: 06_thumbnail/final_A_1929crash_newspaper.jpg)

Use this for EVERY video. Only the 2 words and the prop change.

| Part | Rule (copy into every prompt) |
|---|---|
| Size | 16:9, 1280x720 |
| Background | deep money-green (#0F3D2A), soft old-paper texture, a faint lighter green glow behind the character, darker edges. No scene, no clutter. |
| Text line 1 | huge, very bold, condensed all-caps sans (Impact / Bebas Neue style), white with a slight worn/grunge texture, top left, about 30% of the height |
| Text line 2 | the same font, white letters on a red (#D62728) torn-paper label, slightly tilted, under line 1 |
| Character | our narrator, right half, from the waist up, big (about 75% of the height): round peach-white head, messy spiky brown hair, big round black glasses, bright orange scarf, thin black stick arms, thick black outlines, flat colors |
| Emotion | strong and clear: worried with a sweat drop (default), shocked, or scared |
| Prop | ONE prop held in front of him with a few big readable words that say the topic (like the newspaper "WALL ST. CRASH") |
| Brand mark | a red bookmark ribbon at the top-right corner with small white text "DOLLAR DIARIES" |
| Colors | only green, white, red, orange (scarf), cream/sepia (prop), black outlines |
| Never | extra text, extra objects, logos, busy backgrounds, a smiling face |
