## The winning formula (all 9 videos over 800k use it)
1. **White background**, only 3 things: a short text line, one REAL chart, and one doodle character.
2. **Text: 2–4 words, big bold black serif (Times-like), ending with a period**, top left (sometimes top right).
   It never repeats the title; it adds emotion. Examples:
   - "This Should've Crashed."
   - "It's Happening Again."
   - "Nobody Can Stop It."
   - "It's A Trap."
   - "They're Selling Garbage."
   - "Nobody's Reacting."
   - "History is Repeating"
   - "We Can't Stop It."
   - "The Landing Will Hurt."
   - "Are We Cooked."
   Sometimes one word is red ("Cooked.", "F***D.").
3. **A real chart, simplified**:
   - Grey line for the past, a red line for the recent spike or crash.
   - Big red year labels ("2008" … "2026", "1986" … "2026").
   - Red circles on the peaks and red arrows.
   - A small bold chart headline ("U.S. Homes Spike to Record Unaffordability", "Record Margin Debt Flooding Into Stocks").
   - Or 2 bars compared (SpaceX $1.75T vs Coca-Cola+McDonald's+Disney+Nike+Starbucks $0.91T).
4. **One doodle character reacting**:
   - Most used: a **green USA map with big cartoon eyes** (worried, side-eye, or sunglasses).
   - Others: the stick narrator (round head, spiky black hair, worried), the Fed building with eyes, a top-hat rich man,
     a robot (AI), or a president at a desk.
   - Often a small second character peeks in from the frame edge.
5. Sometimes the chart sits on a **green chalkboard or an easel**, and the narrator points at it with a stick.
6. **History comparison** works: an old year vs today on one chart line.

## What does NOT work (videos under 150k views)
- Illustration-only scenes with no chart and no text (Trojan horse, puppet hand over Wall Street, a drowning Statue of Liberty).
- Busy collages (the investor-types grid).

## Our rule
Every thumbnail = 2–4 word serif text with a period + one real chart (grey past, red key part, red years, red circle)
+ one doodle character with eyes reacting. White background. Nothing else.
Claude builds the chart from real data and puts the text on with a real serif font (AI image tools misspell text);
