## Rules we take
1. Every noun gets a picture on its word: company -> logo, institution -> logo/building, object -> icon, date -> calendar.
2. Proof is a journey: logo -> article headline -> scroll to the quote (highlight) -> scroll to the chart -> figure points.
3. Several proofs for one claim, stacked as cards, about 0.3 s apart.
4. Callbacks: when the voice recaps, the earlier proofs flash back.
5. Idioms become literal gags (secret -> TOP SECRET papers; measuring America -> ruler on the map; all in -> poker chips).
6. Labels are drawn on the charts ("Average: 16%"), not only highlights.
