## The 4 styles that win
1. **Depth scene (Econ sketch, #1, #2, #4):** a detailed, slightly 3D painted background (White House, Capitol,
   flag, storm clouds, dark vignette), ONE big stickman in front (round white head, worried face, suit) holding one
   prop ("DEBT" paper). 1–2 huge words at the top, white or yellow, with a dark shadow. Feels like a movie poster.
2. **Real history photo (#7, #8):** a black-and-white 1929 crowd photo, a newspaper "STOCKS COLLAPSE", 3–5 words in
   white + red at the bottom. Works best for history topics (419x).
3. **Simple chart (#6):** black background, one line chart, red years (2000, 2008, 2026), "WE ARE HERE", red arrow down.
4. **Split comparison (#12):** two stickmen side by side, left sad/poor, right rich, one word above each.

## Common rules
- 1–4 words only, huge, readable on a phone. Never repeat the title.
- One face with a strong emotion (worried, shocked, sweating).
- High contrast: dark background + white/yellow/red text, or a white background + black text.
- One red element that pulls the eye (arrow, circle, red word).
