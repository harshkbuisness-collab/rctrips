## CHAPTER 3: Inside each country
Japan shows why a country can owe an enormous sum largely to its domestic financial system. Domestic banks, insurers, pension managers and the central bank can hold government bonds.

## CHAPTER 6: So can the whole world go bankrupt? Why it is not like a person's debt

## CHAPTER 7: When it does go wrong
The practical lesson is to ask who holds the bonds, in what currency, for how long, and at what rate. Debt is not automatically bad; debts can finance useful services and investments.
