## CHAPTER 3: Bank runs
Bank runs are dangerous because good long-term assets may not turn into spendable cash instantly without losses. A rumor can become self-fulfilling.
> VISUAL: D15 slides in; D16 drops in; C01 pops in; C13 walks in; E21 draws in; red circle; zoom; yellow highlight.
Deposit insurance was developed to protect ordinary savers and reduce incentives to flee together.
> VISUAL: D16 slides in; C01 drops in; C13 pops in; E21 walks in; E08 draws in; red circle; zoom; yellow highlight.

> VISUAL: C01 slides in; C13 drops in; E21 pops in; E08 walks in; D24 draws in; red circle; zoom; yellow highlight.
