Want big shoulders without weights? Push-ups won't do it alone. You need to push overhead. And you can do that on the floor. It's called a pike push-up. Hands on the floor, hips high, so your body makes an upside-down V. Bend your elbows and lower the top of your head toward the floor, between your hands. Then press back up. Now your shoulders do the work, like an overhead press. Six to twelve reps, three sets, twice a week. Too easy? Put your feet on a chair. Send this to the friend with no shoulders.
