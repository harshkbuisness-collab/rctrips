Ten minutes of skipping rope. It's one of the fastest cardio workouts you can do at home. It gets your heart rate up quickly, trains your calves, and improves your coordination. Start easy. Thirty seconds of jumping, thirty seconds of rest, ten rounds. Stay on the balls of your feet with small, quick jumps. No rope? Do the same motion without one.
