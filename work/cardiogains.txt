Cardio won't kill your gains. That's mostly a myth. And skipping it can hold you back. A few cardio sessions a week won't stop you from building muscle. A stronger heart helps you recover between sets and between workouts. It only becomes a problem with huge amounts, like running for hours every day while not eating enough. So do it like this. Two or three sessions a week, twenty to thirty minutes. Walking, cycling, or easy running. Keep your hard cardio away from leg day. Train, eat enough, and you'll keep your muscle.
