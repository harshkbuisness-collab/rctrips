You can't do a single pull-up. That's normal. And there's a simple path to your first one. Step one. Dead hangs. Hang from a bar with straight arms. Build up to thirty seconds. This makes your grip strong enough. Step two. Table rows. Lie under a sturdy table, grab the edge and pull your chest up. This builds your back. Step three. Negatives. Jump to the top of the bar and lower yourself slowly for five seconds. Three sets of each, three times a week. Most people get their first pull-up in four to eight weeks. Send this to the friend who just hangs there. Because you can't do a single pull-up...
