# 60 new scripts (videos 46-105)

Each script is about 25-30 seconds of voice (61-90 words).

## 46. lovehandles (87 words)

You do side crunches for your love handles. They're still there.
You can't burn fat from one spot. Love handles shrink when your overall body fat goes down.
But side planks make the muscles under them stronger and tighter. Lie on your side, elbow under your shoulder, and lift your hips into one straight line. Hold thirty seconds each side, three rounds.
Pair it with a small calorie deficit and daily walks.

## 47. wallsit (88 words)

How long can you hold a wall sit?
This simple test shows how strong your legs really are.
Back flat against a wall. Slide down until your knees are at ninety degrees.
Under thirty seconds: weak. Thirty to sixty: average. One to two minutes: strong. Over two minutes: very strong.
To improve, do three holds a day and add five seconds every few days.
Comment your time. And send this to the friend who says his legs are strong. Because how long can you hold a wall sit...

## 48. deadhang30 (86 words)

Hang from a bar for one minute a day. Thirty days.
Here's what happens.
Week one, your hands give out at twenty seconds. Week two, your grip gets noticeably stronger. By the end of the month, you hang the full minute, your shoulders feel looser, and your first pull-up gets closer.
Grip the bar fully, arms straight, shoulders slightly engaged. Split the minute into short hangs if you need to.
Send this to the friend who can't hang for ten seconds. Because one minute a day...

## 49. skinnycalves (88 words)

Your calves stay skinny no matter what you do.
Walking isn't enough. Calves need full range and slow reps.
Stand on a stair on the balls of your feet. Lower your heels all the way down until you feel a stretch. Pause. Then rise as high as you can and squeeze for one second.
Fifteen to twenty slow reps, three sets, every other day. Too easy? Do one leg at a time.
Send this to the friend hiding his calves in long pants. Because your calves stay skinny...

## 52. abseveryday (84 words)

Training them every day doesn't make them show faster. Lower body fat does that.
So train abs hard two or three times a week. Pick two moves: leg raises and planks. Three sets each, close to failure. Then rest.
On the other days, walk, eat well, and sleep.
