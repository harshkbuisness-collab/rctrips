Diamond push-ups hurt your wrists? Your hands are too close. And too far forward. Squeezing your hands into a tight diamond twists your wrists and pushes your elbows out. So change two things. First, spread your hands a little, about the width of your face, with your fingers pointing forward. Second, put them under your lower chest, not under your face. Keep your elbows tight to your sides on the way down. Same triceps burn. No wrist pain. Eight to fifteen reps, three sets. Still painful? Do them on your knees, or on your fists. Send this to the friend shaking his wrists after push-ups. Because diamond push-ups hurt your wrists...
