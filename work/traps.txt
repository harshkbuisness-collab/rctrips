Want bigger traps without a gym? Your traps are the muscles between your neck and shoulders. They make you look strong from every angle. Hold two heavy water jugs, or a backpack full of books. Shrug your shoulders straight up to your ears. Hold for one second at the top. Lower slowly. Don't roll your shoulders. Fifteen to twenty reps, three sets, twice a week.
