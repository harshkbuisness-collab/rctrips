You flex your biceps, and it looks flat. No peak at all. Part of that is genetics. But most guys are missing one detail that makes a big difference. Your biceps don't just bend your elbow. They also turn your palm up. If you never finish that twist, you're not fully working them. So try chin-ups, with your palms facing you. At the top, squeeze hard and pull your pinky slightly up. Can't do a chin-up? Jump to the top and lower yourself slowly for five seconds. Three sets, twice a week. Send this to the friend with flat biceps. Because you flex your biceps...
