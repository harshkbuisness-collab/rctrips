No pull-up bar? Use your door. Most home workouts skip pulling. This fixes it with one towel. Loop a towel around a sturdy door handle. Hold both ends, feet close to the door, and lean back with straight arms. Pull your chest to the door, squeeze your shoulder blades, and lower slowly. Walk your feet closer to make it harder. Ten to fifteen reps, three sets. Make sure the door is locked. Send this to the friend with no back workout. Because no pull-up bar...
