You can't out-train a bad diet. A thirty-minute workout burns around three hundred calories. Two slices of pizza can be over five hundred. That's why people train hard and still don't lose fat. Training builds muscle and keeps you strong. But fat loss mostly happens in the kitchen. So keep training, and fix your plate: more protein, more vegetables, fewer fried snacks and sugary drinks.
