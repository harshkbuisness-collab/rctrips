Hang from a bar for one minute a day. Thirty days. Here's what happens. Week one, your hands give out at twenty seconds. Week two, your grip gets noticeably stronger. By the end of the month, you hang the full minute, your shoulders feel looser, and your first pull-up gets closer. Grip the bar fully, arms straight, shoulders slightly engaged. Split the minute into short hangs if you need to. Send this to the friend who can't hang for ten seconds. Because one minute a day...
