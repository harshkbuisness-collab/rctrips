You start your workout with curls and crunches, and save push-ups and squats for the end. That order is costing you muscle. And the fix doesn't change a single exercise. Your hardest exercises need your freshest energy. If your arms are already tired from curls, you'll do fewer push-ups and dips. Fewer reps on the big exercises means less growth. So follow this order. First, the hardest exercises that use the most muscle: squats, push-ups, pull-ups, rows. Then single-muscle exercises like curls and lateral raises. Core work at the very end.
