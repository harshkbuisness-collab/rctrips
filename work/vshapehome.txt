You train at home, but your shoulders and back look exactly the same as last year. Most home workouts are all push-ups. And a V-shape needs pulling too. For a V-shape, you need wide shoulders on top and a wide back under them. Push-ups alone build your chest, but they don't build width. So here's a full V-shape workout. Pike push-ups for your shoulders, eight to twelve reps. Table rows for your back, ten to fifteen reps. Reverse snow angels lying face down, twelve slow reps, for your upper back and rear shoulders. Three rounds, three times a week. Send this to the friend whose home workout is only push-ups. Because you train at home...
