Crunches hurt your neck? You're pulling your head with your hands. Your neck does the work, not your abs. So fix it. Hands lightly on your chest, never pulling. Keep a fist of space between your chin and chest. Look at the ceiling. Curl up by squeezing your abs, lifting only your shoulder blades. Lower slowly. Twelve to fifteen slow reps, three sets.
