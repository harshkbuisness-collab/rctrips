One arm is bigger than the other. That's normal. And it's easy to even out. Your dominant arm does more all day, so it's often bigger. In push-ups and two-arm curls, the strong side quietly takes over. So train one arm at a time. Hammer curls with a water jug or dumbbell. Always start with your weaker arm. Count the reps, then do exactly the same number with your strong arm. Not one more. Three sets per arm, twice a week. In a few months, the gap gets much smaller. Send this to the friend who only flexes one arm in photos. Because one arm is bigger...
