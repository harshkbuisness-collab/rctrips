Can't do a single push-up? You're not weak. You're just starting at the wrong level. Here's the ladder. Step one. Wall push-ups. Hands on a wall, body straight, lower your chest to the wall. When you can do twenty, move on. Step two. Incline push-ups. Hands on a table or a bed. Same straight body. Twenty clean reps, then move on. Step three. Lowering. From the top of a push-up, lower yourself slowly for five seconds. Then drop your knees and get back up. Three sets, every other day. Most people get their first push-up in a few weeks.
