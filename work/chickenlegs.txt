Big upper body. Chicken legs. Bodyweight squats stop working fast. Your legs need more load. So do Bulgarian split squats. Back foot on a chair, front foot forward. Lower straight down until your back knee almost touches the floor. Push through your front heel. All your bodyweight goes on one leg, so it's like adding weight for free. Eight to twelve reps per leg, three sets, twice a week. Send this to the friend who skips leg day. Because big upper body...
