Your phone is changing your posture. Rounded shoulders. Head forward. And it makes you look smaller. Hours of looking down make your chest tight and your upper back weak. Your shoulders roll forward and your head follows. Two fixes, every day. One, chin tucks. Pull your chin straight back, like making a double chin. Hold five seconds. Ten reps. Two, wall angels. Back against a wall, arms up like a goalpost. Slide your arms up and down, keeping them on the wall. Ten slow reps. And hold your phone at eye level. Send this to the friend who's always looking down. Because your phone is changing your posture...
