Your belly sticks out, but you're not fat. It might be your posture, not your fat. And two exercises help. When you sit all day, the front of your hips gets tight and your glutes get weak. Your pelvis tips forward, your lower back arches, and your stomach gets pushed out. So do this. Glute bridges: squeeze your glutes hard at the top for two seconds. Fifteen reps. Then planks: tuck your hips and squeeze your glutes. Thirty seconds. Three rounds, every day. And stand up and stretch your hips every hour. Send this to the friend who's always sucking his belly in.
