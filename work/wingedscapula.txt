Look at your back in the mirror. If your shoulder blades stick out like wings, this is for you. It's a weak muscle, not bad bones. And the fix is a push-up you're not doing. The serratus holds your shoulder blades flat against your ribs. When it's weak, your shoulder blades lift away, your posture looks off, and push-ups feel less stable. So do scapular push-ups. Get into a push-up position with straight arms. Without bending your elbows, let your chest sink so your shoulder blades come together, then push the floor away so they spread apart. Fifteen reps, three sets, three times a week. Send this to the friend whose shoulder blades stick out. Because look at your back in the mirror...
