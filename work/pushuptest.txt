How many push-ups can you do right now, in one set, with perfect form? Be honest. Because your number says a lot about your strength. Here's a rough guide. Full reps only, chest close to the floor, body straight. Under ten: you're a beginner. Ten to twenty: average. Twenty to thirty-five: strong. Over forty: very strong. Whatever your number is, here's how to raise it. Three hard sets, three times a week, stopping just before failure. Add one rep to each set every week. Comment your number. And send this to the friend who says he can do fifty. Because how many push-ups can you do...
