Wide grip or close grip pull-ups? Both train your back. But they feel different. Wide grip, palms away: more upper back and lats. It's harder, so you'll do fewer reps. Close grip, palms facing you: these are chin-ups. More biceps, easier, more reps. Beginners should start with chin-ups to build strength, then add wide grip. The best plan? Do both every week.
