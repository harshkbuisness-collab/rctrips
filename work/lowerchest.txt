Your lower chest looks flat. Incline push-ups, with your hands up high, hit your lower chest more. Put your hands on a table, bed or kitchen counter. Body straight. Lower your chest to the edge, then press back up. Squeeze your chest at the top. Go slow: three seconds down. Fifteen to twenty reps, three sets. Mix them with dips and normal push-ups for a full chest. Send this to the friend whose chest has no shape. Because your lower chest looks flat...
