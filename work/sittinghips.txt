You sit for eight hours a day. Your hips pay for it. And your lower back too. When you sit, the muscles at the front of your hips stay short all day. Over time they get tight, and they pull your pelvis forward. That's why your lower back feels stiff when you stand up. So do this every day. Kneel on one knee, like proposing.
