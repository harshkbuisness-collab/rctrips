Your heels lift when you squat. It's your ankles, not your legs. And it takes two minutes a day to fix. When your ankles are stiff, your knees can't travel forward. So your heels pop up and you tip forward. Fix one, right now. Stand a little wider and turn your toes out. Your heels will stay down. Fix two, every day. Kneel in a lunge, front foot flat. Push your front knee forward over your toes without lifting the heel. Hold thirty seconds each side. Do it daily for a few weeks and your squat will get deeper. Send this to the friend who squats on his toes. Because your heels lift when you squat...
