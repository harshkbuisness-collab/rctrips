Calisthenics or the gym? Both build muscle. Here's the real difference. Your muscles don't know if it's a dumbbell or your body weight. They only know how hard the work is. The gym makes it easy to add a little weight every week. That's great for legs and for beginners who need easy steps. Calisthenics is free, works anywhere, and builds control and strength through your whole body. But you have to keep making the exercises harder. So pick the one you'll actually do. Or mix both.
