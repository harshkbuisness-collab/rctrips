Knee push-ups aren't cheating. If you can't do ten good push-ups, knee push-ups build your chest better than ugly full ones. Keep your body straight from your knees to your head. Hands just outside your shoulders. Chest close to the floor. Do them until you can hit twenty clean reps. Then mix in full push-ups. Bad form on full push-ups won't build anything. Send this to the friend who laughs at knee push-ups. Because knee push-ups aren't cheating...
