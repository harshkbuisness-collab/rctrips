Your hands give out before your muscles do. On pull-ups, on carrying bags, everywhere. Your grip is the weak link. And these two exercises fix it without any gym. There are different types of grip. Holding heavy things for a long time, and pinching with your fingers and thumb. Most people train neither. Exercise one. Farmer carries: pick up two heavy bags or water jugs and walk until your grip is about to give out. Three rounds. Exercise two. Book pinches: hold two heavy books together, spines out, pinched between your fingers and thumb. Hold as long as you can. Three rounds per hand. Send this to the friend with the weakest handshake.
