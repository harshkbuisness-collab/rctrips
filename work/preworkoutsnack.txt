You train on an empty stomach and feel weak. Your body needs fuel for a hard workout. Eat a small snack thirty to sixty minutes before. A banana. Bread with peanut butter. Or curd with fruit. Easy carbs for energy, a bit of protein, and nothing heavy or oily. Drink a glass of water too. If you train early in the morning, a banana is enough. Send this to the friend who almost faints doing squats. Because you train on an empty stomach...
