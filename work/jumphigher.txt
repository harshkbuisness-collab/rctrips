You want to jump higher. You need strong legs and fast legs. Strong: squats and split squats, slow and controlled. Fast: squat jumps. Squat down halfway, then explode up as high as you can. Land softly with bent knees, reset, and jump again. Five jumps, five sets, full rest between sets. Quality over quantity. Twice a week, and you'll feel the difference in a few weeks. Send this to the friend who can't touch the ceiling. Because you want to jump higher...
