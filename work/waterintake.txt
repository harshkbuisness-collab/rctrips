Eight glasses of water a day isn't a magic number. How much you need depends on your body, the weather, and how much you sweat. Easy check: look at your pee. Pale yellow means you're fine. Dark yellow means drink more. For most active people, that's around two to three liters a day, more on hot days and workout days. Keep a bottle with you and sip all day.
