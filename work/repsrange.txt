How many reps should you do to build muscle? Anywhere from six to thirty reps can build muscle. What matters is how close you get to failure. The last two or three reps should feel really hard. Twenty easy push-ups and stopping fresh won't do much. So pick a rep range, push close to failure, and add a rep or a harder version each week. Three to four hard sets per muscle.
