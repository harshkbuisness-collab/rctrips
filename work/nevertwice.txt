Missed a workout? Never miss two in a row. That one rule keeps most people consistent. Missing one day is normal. Life happens. But missing two days is the start of a new habit: the habit of not training. So if you missed yesterday, do something today. Even ten push-ups and a short walk. Your streak is about showing up, not being perfect. Send this to the friend who quits after one missed day. Because never miss two in a row...
