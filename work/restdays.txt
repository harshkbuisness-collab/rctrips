Training every day isn't making you bigger. Your muscles grow on your rest days. Not during your workout. Training creates small damage in your muscles. When you rest, eat and sleep, your body repairs them a little stronger. Train the same muscles hard every day, and they never get time to repair. You feel tired and your strength stops going up. So train each muscle hard two or three times a week, with a day of rest in between. On rest days, walk, stretch, and sleep well. Rest is part of the plan.
