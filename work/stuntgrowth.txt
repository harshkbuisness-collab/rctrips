Working out won't stunt your growth. That's an old myth. Your height is mostly decided by genetics, sleep, and nutrition. Studies show strength training is safe for teens when the form is good and the weights are sensible. The real risk is bad form and showing off with weights that are too heavy. So start with bodyweight, learn proper technique, and sleep well. Send this to the friend who's scared to train at sixteen. Because working out won't stunt your growth...
