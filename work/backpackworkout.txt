Your school bag is a full gym. Fill a backpack with books and you have adjustable weight. Backpack squats: hug it to your chest and squat deep. Backpack rows: hinge forward and pull it to your belly. Backpack presses: lie on the floor and press it up like a bench press. Twelve reps each, three rounds. Add more books when it gets easy. Send this to the friend who says he has no weights.
