Your legs feel weak, and climbing stairs leaves you out of breath. Fifty squats a day can change that in a month. Here's exactly what happens. Week one, your legs get sore. Week two, you feel more energy. Week three, stairs feel easy and your hips feel less stiff. Week four, your legs are stronger and you've built a daily habit. But here's the catch. Once fifty squats feel easy, they stop building muscle. So after thirty days, slow down to three seconds on the way down, add a pause at the bottom, or switch to Bulgarian split squats.
