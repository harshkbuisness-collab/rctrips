Your knees can go past your toes. That old rule is mostly a myth. Here's what actually hurts knees. To squat deep, your knees have to travel forward. For most healthy people, that's completely normal. The real problem is your knees caving inward. That's what stresses them. So squat like this. Feet shoulder-width apart, toes slightly out. Push your knees out in the same direction as your toes. Keep your whole foot on the floor. Sit down between your heels, chest up. Pain? Go less deep and build up slowly.
