Your lateral raises aren't working. You're swinging too heavy. And your traps are stealing the work. When the weight is too heavy, you shrug and swing. Your neck muscles take over and your side shoulders barely work. So fix it. Go lighter. Water bottles are enough. Lean slightly forward. Lift with your elbows, not your hands, out to the sides. Stop at shoulder height. Lower slowly for three seconds. Twelve to twenty reps, three sets, twice a week. It should burn on the side of your shoulder, not your neck.
