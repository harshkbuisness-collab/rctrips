Sweating doesn't burn fat. That puddle on the floor is water. Not fat. Sweat is how your body cools down. Some people sweat a lot, some barely sweat. It doesn't show how hard you worked. Wearing a jacket or a sweat suit makes you lose water weight. You drink, and it comes right back. What actually burns fat? Eating a little less than you burn, every day. Training hard to keep your muscle. And walking more. So drop the sweat suit. Drink water and train. Send this to the friend wearing a hoodie in summer. Because sweating doesn't burn fat...
