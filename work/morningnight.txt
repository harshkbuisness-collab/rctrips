Morning or night. Which workout is better? The honest answer. And the one rule that matters more. In the evening, your body is warmer and most people are a bit stronger. In the morning, it's easier to be consistent, because the day hasn't got in the way yet. But the difference is small. What matters is training at a time you can repeat every week. So pick your time. Morning? Warm up longer. Night? Finish at least an hour or two before bed, and keep it lighter if it ruins your sleep. The best workout time is the one you don't skip.
