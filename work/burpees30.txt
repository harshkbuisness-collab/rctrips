Burpees. Every day. Thirty days. The exercise everybody hates. Here's why that's the point. A burpee is a squat, a push-up and a jump in one. It works your whole body and gets your heart pounding in seconds. Start with ten a day. Add two every few days. By the end of the month, your stamina is noticeably better and stairs feel like nothing. Keep it safe. Land softly on your feet. Keep your body straight in the push-up. Too hard? Step back instead of jumping, and skip the jump at the top. Send this to the friend who says he's fit. Because burpees...
