You've built your shoulders, but your body still doesn't look V-shaped. The problem is at the bottom of the V. Your waist. And there's an exercise most people have never tried. Your deep core muscle wraps around your stomach like a belt. When it's weak, your stomach pushes out, even if you're not fat. So try stomach vacuums. Breathe all the air out. Then pull your belly button in toward your spine as hard as you can. Hold for fifteen to twenty seconds while breathing lightly. Five rounds a day. It won't burn fat, but it trains the muscle that holds your stomach in. Send this to the friend building shoulders with no V. Because you've built your shoulders...
