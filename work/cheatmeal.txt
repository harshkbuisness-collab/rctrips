You ate pizza last night. Now you think your diet is ruined. It isn't. One meal doesn't erase a week of good eating. Fat gain comes from what you do most days, not one day. The scale might jump tomorrow, but that's mostly water and salt. The real damage is the thought: I already ruined it, so why stop now? So enjoy it, drink water, and eat normally at the next meal. Send this to the friend who quits his diet after one burger. Because you ate pizza last night...
