The first calisthenics skill anyone can learn. It's called the crow pose. And it takes balance more than strength. Squat down, hands flat on the floor, shoulder-width. Bend your elbows a little and rest your knees on the back of your upper arms. Look forward, not down. Slowly lean forward until your feet lift off the floor.
