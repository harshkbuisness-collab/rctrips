Buying your first dumbbells? Don't buy one light pair. You'll outgrow it in a month. Buy an adjustable pair if you can. One pair that goes from light to heavy covers your whole body for years. For most beginners, a set that goes up to fifteen or twenty kilos per hand is enough. If you buy fixed ones, get a light pair and a heavy pair. Send this to the friend with tiny two-kilo dumbbells.
