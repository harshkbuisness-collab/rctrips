You want bigger forearms, but you don't have any weights at home. You don't need them. Your fingers and the floor are enough. Exercise one. Fingertip planks. Get into a push-up position on your fingertips instead of flat palms. Hold for fifteen to twenty seconds. This puts huge tension through your fingers, wrists and forearms. Exercise two. Knuckle push-ups, on a soft mat or carpet. Make fists and do push-ups on your knuckles with straight wrists. It strengthens your wrists and forearms at the same time. Three sets each, three times a week. Start on your knees if it's too hard. Send this to the friend who says he needs dumbbells first. Because you want bigger forearms...
