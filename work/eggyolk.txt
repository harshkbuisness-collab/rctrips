You throw away the egg yolks. Stop. That's where most of the nutrients are. The yolk has vitamins, healthy fats, and almost half the egg's protein. For most healthy people, eating whole eggs every day is fine. So eat whole eggs. Two or three a day, with a few extra whites if you want more protein. Boiled, scrambled, or in an omelette.
