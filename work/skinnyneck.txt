Big body. Skinny neck. Your neck is a muscle too. And almost nobody trains it. Start gentle. Lie on your back on a bed with your head off the edge. Slowly lift your chin to your chest, then lower. Then lie face down and lift your head back. Fifteen slow reps each way, two sets, twice a week. No jerking, no weights at first. Stop if anything hurts.
