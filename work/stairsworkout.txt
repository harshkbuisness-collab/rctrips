Your stairs are a free gym. Climbing stairs builds your legs and your heart at the same time. Here's a stairs workout. Walk up fast, walk down slow. Five rounds. Then step-ups, ten each leg, holding the railing if you need. Then calf raises on the edge of a step, twenty reps. Twice a week, and your legs and stamina get a lot stronger. Send this to the friend who waits ten minutes for the lift. Because your stairs are a free gym...
