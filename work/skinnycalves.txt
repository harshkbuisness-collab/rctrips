Your calves stay skinny no matter what you do. Walking isn't enough. Calves need full range and slow reps. Stand on a stair on the balls of your feet. Lower your heels all the way down until you feel a stretch. Pause. Then rise as high as you can and squeeze for one second. Fifteen to twenty slow reps, three sets, every other day. Too easy? Do one leg at a time. Send this to the friend hiding his calves in long pants. Because your calves stay skinny...
