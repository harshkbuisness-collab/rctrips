You think you have a slow metabolism. Most people don't. People of the same size burn a surprisingly similar amount at rest. The bigger difference is daily movement. Steps, standing, walking around. That can change hundreds of calories a day. And most people eat more than they think. Snacks, drinks, oil, bites while cooking. So walk more, track your food for a week, and build muscle.
