Rounded shoulders? Grab a towel. Hold a towel in front of you with both hands, arms straight, at shoulder height. Pull your hands apart as hard as you can, like you're trying to rip it. Squeeze your shoulder blades together. Hold for five seconds. Ten reps, a few times a day. It wakes up your upper back and pulls your shoulders back. Send this to the friend with the hunched back.
