You train your forearms, but from the side, they still look thin. You're only training half of them. And the other half takes one exercise. The muscle on top of your forearm, near your elbow, is what makes forearms look thick from the side. Wrist curls and grippers barely touch it. So do reverse curls. Hold a loaded backpack or water jug with your palms facing down. Keep your elbows at your sides and curl the weight up to your chest. Lower slowly. Twelve to fifteen reps, three sets, twice a week. You'll feel the top of your forearm burning. Send this to the friend whose forearms look thin from the side. Because you train your forearms...
