You want a muscle-up. First, you need strong pull-ups. Not five. At least ten clean ones. Then train explosive pull-ups. Pull as fast as you can, until your chest reaches the bar. Then practice straight bar dips to learn the top part. Pull high, lean forward, push down. Most people need months, not weeks. That's normal.
