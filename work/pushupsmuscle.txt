Can push-ups alone build muscle? Yes. But only if you stop doing the same push-up forever. Muscle grows when the work gets harder over time. Once thirty push-ups feel easy, they stop working. So make them harder. One, raise your feet on a chair. More weight goes on your chest and shoulders. Two, go slow. Three seconds down, one second up. Three, bring your hands close for your triceps. Three hard sets, three times a week, close to failure. One more thing. Push-ups train your front. Add rows or pull-ups for your back. Send this to the friend who thinks you need a gym to grow.
