Your squats only go halfway down. Half squats skip the part that builds your glutes and legs. Go down until your thighs are at least parallel to the floor. Lower if your heels stay down and your back stays straight. Can't go that low? Squat to a chair, touch it, and stand back up. Use a lower chair over time. Deep squats are safe for healthy knees when your form is good. Send this to the friend doing half squats. Because your squats only go halfway down...
