Eating late at night makes you fat. That's mostly a myth. Your body doesn't store food differently after eight PM. Fat gain comes from eating more than you burn over the whole day. The real problem with late eating is what we eat. Chips, sweets, and second dinners in front of the TV. So if you're hungry at night, eat something light with protein. Curd, eggs, or a glass of milk.
