Your upper chest looks flat. Normal push-ups hit the middle of your chest. The top gets less work. When your feet are higher than your hands, your arms push more upward, like an incline press. That puts more work on your upper chest and front shoulders. So do decline push-ups. Feet on a chair or a bed. Hands on the floor, just outside your shoulders. Body straight. Lower until your nose almost touches the floor, then press up. Eight to fifteen reps, three sets, three times a week. Send this to the friend whose chest stops at his nipples.
