Your shoulders look round from the front, but flat from the side. You're missing the back of your shoulders. Almost nobody trains them. So do reverse flies. Hold two water bottles. Hinge forward with a flat back. Lift your arms out to the sides, thumbs down, and squeeze your shoulder blades. Lower slowly. They make your shoulders look fuller and pull them back. Fifteen to twenty reps, three sets, twice a week. Send this to the friend with flat shoulders. Because your shoulders look round...
