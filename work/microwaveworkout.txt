Waiting for the microwave? Do squats. It's called habit stacking. You attach a small exercise to something you already do every day. Microwave running: squats. Brushing your teeth: calf raises. Water boiling: push-ups against the counter. Before every shower: a thirty-second plank. No extra time needed, and it adds up to hundreds of reps a week. Send this to the friend who says he has no time to work out. Because waiting for the microwave...
