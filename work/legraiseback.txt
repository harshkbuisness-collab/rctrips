Leg raises hurt your lower back? That means your abs aren't doing the work. Your hip muscles are. When your lower back arches off the floor, your hip flexors pull on your spine. Your abs barely switch on. So fix it. Hands under your hips. Press your lower back flat into the floor. Bend your knees a little. Lift your legs slowly, and lower them only as far as your back stays flat. Lift your hips slightly at the top. Ten to fifteen slow reps, three sets. Send this to the friend whose back hurts after abs. Because leg raises hurt your lower back...
