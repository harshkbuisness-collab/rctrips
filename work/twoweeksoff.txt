You stopped working out for two weeks. Relax. You didn't lose your progress. In the first week, nothing much changes. Your muscles may look a bit flatter because they hold less water and stored energy. That's not muscle loss. Your strength stays for a few weeks. Stamina drops first, so cardio feels harder. And when you come back, muscle memory helps. You get back to where you were much faster than the first time. So don't panic. Start again at about seventy percent, and in a couple of weeks you're back. Send this to the friend who thinks he lost everything. Because you stopped working out...
