How much protein do you need? Not as much as supplement ads say. Here's the number. For building muscle, about one point six grams per kilo of body weight per day is enough for most people. Weigh seventy kilos? Around a hundred and ten grams a day. More than that doesn't build more muscle. It's just extra food. So split it across your meals. Eggs, milk, curd, paneer, lentils, chicken or fish. Every meal should have one protein food. Protein powder is fine, but it's just food. Not magic.
