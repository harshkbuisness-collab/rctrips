Your knees crack every time you squat. Relax. In most cases, it's harmless. Those sounds are usually gas bubbles or tendons moving over bone. If there's no pain or swelling, it's normal. Strong legs protect your knees. Keep squatting with good form: knees following your toes, whole foot on the floor, controlled speed. But if the cracking comes with pain, swelling or locking, see a doctor. Send this to the friend whose knees sound like popcorn. Because your knees crack...
