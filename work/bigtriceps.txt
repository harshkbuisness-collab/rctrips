You train biceps for bigger arms. But your triceps make up about two thirds of your upper arm. So if you want bigger arms, train the back of them. Chair dips: hands on a chair, lower until your upper arms are parallel to the floor, then press up. Diamond push-ups: hands close under your chest, elbows tight. Ten to fifteen reps each, three sets, twice a week. Send this to the friend who only does curls. Because you train biceps...
