From the front you look okay. But from behind, you look narrow. Your lats aren't growing. And you don't need a pull-up bar to fix it. Your lats are the big muscles on the sides of your back. They create width from behind. And they only grow from pulling exercises, which most home workouts skip. So here's what to do. Table rows: lie under a sturdy table, grab the edge, pull your chest up, elbows toward your hips. Ten to fifteen reps. Then superman pulls: lie face down, arms forward, pull your elbows down to your ribs while lifting your chest. Three sets each, three times a week. Send this to the friend who never trains back. Because from the front, you look okay...
