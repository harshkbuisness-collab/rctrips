You want to do a handstand. Don't start by kicking up in the middle of the room. Start at a wall. Step one. Pike push-ups to build strong shoulders. Step two. Wall walks. Feet on the wall, walk your hands closer as your feet climb up. Hold for ten seconds. Step three. Chest-to-wall handstand holds, thirty seconds. Practice five minutes a day, on a soft floor.
