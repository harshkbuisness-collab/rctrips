Ten thousand steps a day. Is it worth it? The number isn't magic. But walking is underrated. Ten thousand started as a marketing number. Studies show most of the health benefit comes by about seven or eight thousand steps. Walking burns extra energy without making you tired for your workouts. It helps with fat loss, digestion and stress. And it won't eat your muscle. So take your current steps and add two thousand. Walk after meals, take calls standing up, and use the stairs.
