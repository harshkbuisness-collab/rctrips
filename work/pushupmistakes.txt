You do push-ups every day, and your chest still looks flat. You already know about flared elbows. But there are three more mistakes almost everyone makes. And each one takes two seconds to fix. Mistake one. Sagging hips. Your lower back takes the load, not your chest. Squeeze your glutes and stay in one straight line. Mistake two. Half reps. If your chest isn't close to the floor, you're skipping the part that builds muscle. Mistake three. Hands too far forward. Put them under your chest, just outside your shoulders. Send this to the friend who does fifty half reps. Because you do push-ups every day...
