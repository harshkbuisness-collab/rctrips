Your lower back aches after sitting all day. Often, your back muscles are just weak. Supermans make them stronger. Lie face down, arms forward. Lift your arms, chest and legs a few centimeters off the floor. Squeeze your glutes and hold for three seconds. Lower slowly. Twelve reps, three sets, every other day. Pair it with walking and planks. If the pain is sharp or goes down your leg, see a doctor. Send this to the friend with the stiff back. Because your lower back aches...
