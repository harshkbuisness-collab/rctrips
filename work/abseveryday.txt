You train abs every single day. Abs are muscles. They need rest to grow, just like your arms. Training them every day doesn't make them show faster. Lower body fat does that. So train abs hard two or three times a week. Pick two moves: leg raises and planks. Three sets each, close to failure. Then rest. On the other days, walk, eat well, and sleep.
