Chair dips hurting your shoulders? You're going too deep. And your shoulders are rolling forward. When you drop too low, the front of your shoulder takes all the stress. That's where the pain comes from. So fix it. Hands on the chair, fingers forward. Shoulders down and back, chest up. Lower only until your upper arms are parallel to the floor. Keep your back close to the chair. Then press up through your palms. Bend your knees to make it easier. Ten to fifteen reps, three sets. Send this to the friend whose shoulders click on dips.
