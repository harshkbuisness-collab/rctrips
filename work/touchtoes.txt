Can't touch your toes? Most people stretch their hamstrings the wrong way. Don't bounce and don't round your back. Sit on the floor, one leg straight. Hinge forward from your hips with a flat back until you feel the stretch behind your knee. Hold thirty seconds. Breathe out and reach a little further. Three times each leg, every day. Many people touch their toes within a few weeks. Send this to the friend who can't reach past his knees. Because can't touch your toes...
