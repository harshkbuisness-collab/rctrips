You eat a lot, but you stay skinny. You're probably not eating as much as you think. Here's the fix. Most skinny guys eat one big meal and skip the rest. Over a week, it's not enough to grow. So make it easy. Eat three meals and two snacks. Add calorie-dense foods: milk, peanut butter, bananas, eggs, rice. Drink a glass of milk with every meal. Weigh yourself every week. Gaining nothing? Add a bit more. And train. Without training, the extra food becomes fat instead of muscle. Push-ups, squats and rows, three times a week. Send this to the skinny friend who eats once a day.
