Your curls are too fast. You swing the weight up and drop it down. Your biceps barely work. Slow down. Elbows pinned to your sides. Curl up in one second, squeeze at the top, then lower for three full seconds. The lowering part builds a lot of muscle, and most people skip it. Use a lighter weight. A water jug is enough. Ten to twelve slow reps, three sets. Send this to the friend swinging curls in the mirror. Because your curls are too fast...
